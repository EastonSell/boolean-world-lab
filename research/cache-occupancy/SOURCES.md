# Prior-art and novelty ledger
Reviewed 2026-10-27; search is not exhaustive. No novelty certification.

- Baseline exact cache semantics and coarse deterministic bound: [AMORTIZATION.md at 23fc37a](https://github.com/EastonSell/boolean-world-lab/blob/23fc37a/docs/AMORTIZATION.md), and [comparison.py](https://github.com/EastonSell/boolean-world-lab/blob/23fc37a/comparison.py). This note sharpens the analysis of that particular uncompressed prefix cache; it does not replace its counter.
- Achlioptas, Hammoudeh and Theodoropoulos, *Fast Sampling of Perfectly Uniform Satisfying Assignments*, SAT 2018: [authors' SPUR repository](https://github.com/ZaydH/spur), directly inspected. Establishes prior practical exact uniform SAT sampling, with caching and batches. Our theorem is not an algorithmic priority claim over SPUR and our checks are not a performance comparison.
- Sharma, Gupta, Roy and Meel, *Knowledge Compilation meets Uniform Sampling*, 2018: [author publication page](https://rahulguptakota.github.io/publication/lpar18/) found via search; direct fetch failed. The indexed author abstract states KUS compiles constraints to d-DNNF and samples through the compiled representation. This is prior art against portraying repeated exact sampling via compilation as new. Full paper not reviewed in this subtask.
- *Data structure set-trie for storing and querying sets: Theoretical and empirical analysis*, 2021: [paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7875400/) search extracts show node-occupancy probabilities and sums of node expectations under random set inclusion. Direct open encountered a browser challenge. This is closely related occupancy analysis under a different randomization model, not evidence that the proposed finite-support extremal envelope is new. Full paper must be checked before priority assertions.
- *Analytic Approach to Pattern Matching*, chapter draft dated May 18, 2004: [author-hosted PDF](https://www.cs.purdue.edu/homes/spa/papers/lothaire.pdf), directly opened; section 7.6 discusses random tries, source distributions and prefix-based analysis. Trie occupancy analysis substantially predates this project. This note uses elementary exact finite-support reasoning rather than claiming a new theory of tries.

Search terms included: exact uniform sampling satisfying assignments amortized multiple samples trie counting oracle; trie expected number internal nodes finite set sampling occupancy extremal balanced; prefix tree expected distinct prefixes fixed number strings extremal occupancy; trie majorization; occupancy Schur concave distinct; bit reversal trie balanced. Absence of a matching search result is not evidence of priority. Concavity/majorization and bit reversal are standard methods.

## Defensible current description
An elementary, independently reviewable refinement of the project's cache analysis, including sharp globally attainable bounds and a variable-order limitation. The all-depth realizability argument is explicit instead of assuming that independently optimal level counts form a valid trie. Literature novelty remains unestablished. No practical speedup, real-world efficacy, or general lower bound for exact samplers is claimed.
