# What this construction can and cannot establish

## Literature first
This study applies known mathematics rather than proposing a new complexity
result. Primary sources inspected before protocol development:

- [Valiant (1979), The Complexity of Enumeration and Reliability Problems](https://epubs.siam.org/doi/abs/10.1137/0208032): decision/existence and counting are different tasks; many natural counting problems are #P-complete.
- [Darwiche and Marquis (2002), A Knowledge Compilation Map](https://mlanthology.org/jair/2002/darwiche2002jair-knowledge/): compact representation, preprocessing, permitted queries and conditioning must be considered together. Cheap queries after expensive compilation are not free end-to-end simulation.
- [Chakraborty et al. (2014), Distribution-Aware Sampling and Weighted Model Counting for SAT](https://arxiv.org/abs/1404.2984): counting and distribution-aware generation are established, assumption-sensitive inference tasks.
- [Dawson et al. (2005), Quantum computing and polynomial equations over Z2](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0408129): quantum computation already has a signed counting/path-sum formulation. Representing amplitudes by counts is not by itself a new efficient simulator.
- [Markov and Shi (2008), Simulating quantum computation by contracting tensor networks](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0511069): structural width provides a relevant classical simulation parameter, rather than qubit number alone.
- [Pashayan, Wallman and Bartlett (2015)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1503.07525): quasiprobability negativity controls their sampling-estimation cost; cancellation must be measured rather than hidden.
- [Bostrom (2003), Are You Living in a Computer Simulation?](https://simulation-argument.com/simulation/): simulation-frequency conclusions depend on substantive assumptions about civilizations, observers and reference classes. A miniature-world experiment supplies none of these quantities.

The existing Boolean World Lab SOURCES.md separately discusses the 2026
OpenAI manuscript/formal-scope claims. This extension does not independently
verify that theorem or execute its algorithm. Neither a subexponential bound
nor P=NP would on its own establish practical exact counting, conscious
simulation, or the ontological simulation hypothesis.

## Three separate claims
1. Logical finite emulation: a specified finite computable dynamics can be
executed or represented as a finite distribution of histories.
2. Budgeted feasibility: a named implementation meets a stated time/memory/
output-accuracy contract on stated instances and hardware.
3. Actual-universe simulation: our observed reality is generated by an
external host. Neither 1 nor 2 entails 3.

The observer in this experiment has sensing, persistent memory, and feedback.
It does not claim learning, biological complexity, consciousness, or a
validated measure of observerhood. More elaborate finite policies can be
unrolled into circuits, but their extra gates and tape randomness must count.
A prewritten narrative alone would not demonstrate interactive feedback;
this study instead computes every transition from the same fixed policy.

## Elementary transcript-equivalence proposition
Let U be n independent fair bits, let deterministic finite dynamics plus an
observer policy map U to transcript Y=f(U), and let E(U) be a fixed event.
Let K be the number of tapes satisfying E. If K>0, choose a tape uniformly
among the K accepted tapes by exact rank self-reduction. Then for every y,

  P(Y=y | E) = #{u : E(u) and f(u)=y}/K.

Proof: accepted tapes each have probability 1/K; sum over the disjoint tapes
mapping to y. A parsimonious gate-equivalence encoding gives exactly one
auxiliary assignment per tape, so CNF model counts equal those tape counts.
Unused inputs still multiply counts. Multiple tapes with the same transcript
must not be collapsed into one equiprobable transcript. This is the reason to
compare full multiplicity maps, not only sets of possible observations.
For K=0 no conditional distribution exists.

An online transcript-only generator is also equivalent if it uses correct
conditional masses for every reachable prefix: for prefix p, select next
symbol a with mass W(pa)/W(p). Chain multiplication telescopes to W(y)/W(empty).
Zero-weight prefixes must never be entered. For adaptive observers, the policy
and action history must be part of the model; independently answering mutually
inconsistent questions does not implement one coherent world. The present
implementation samples full tapes and replays them; it does NOT implement or
benchmark an online transcript-only/lazy generator.

## Why this is not evidence of an external host
If two hypotheses induce the identical distribution on every accessible
observer transcript, any test of those transcripts has the same outcome
probabilities under both. For a transcript with nonzero likelihood, their
likelihood ratio is 1; Bayes' rule leaves their prior odds unchanged. This is
an elementary observational-equivalence result, not a claim that all possible
simulation hypotheses are unfalsifiable. A specific resource-limited host
model might predict deviations; it must specify them before data collection
and compete against ordinary physical explanations.

Likewise, finding a compatible history proves existence within a chosen model,
not high prior probability. Conditioning deliberately on observer existence or
an interesting outcome cannot then be presented as evidence that this outcome
is typical. Report P(E), conditional multiplicities and all prior assumptions.
There is no canonical uniform distribution over all possible universes or
programs supplied by these repositories.

## Host-resource accounting
For an explicit n-bit input/g-gate circuit, N=n+g variables and at most 4g+1
clauses suffice here. Input-tape enumeration takes 2^n evaluations; a single
forward execution takes O(n+g) elementary work and need not enumerate possible
worlds. Exact conditioning may amortize for repeated rare-event draws, but its
full preprocessing/counting bill must be paid and compared with rejection
sampling's expected 1/P(E) proposals under the SAME requested contract.
Our bounded experiment instead uses exact-count and full-support contracts,
so it does not assert a rare-event sampling speedup.

Record host seconds and bytes, not invented joules or simulated physical
seconds. Wall time need not match internal simulated time. A sparse local
observer can reduce necessary work only where a proved dependency/causal-cone
argument preserves all requested observables and feedback. A retrospective
small cone is not automatically valid for arbitrary future interventions.
A large constant or degree can defeat practical feasibility even when an
asymptotic algorithm is polynomial; N also includes all unrolled time steps.
No extrapolation to atoms, cosmological volumes, Planck cells, quantum gravity,
or conscious human populations is licensed by this finite model.

## Quantum connection and next bounded study
Quantum-signed-paths addresses a different layer: exact Born probabilities
from interference. A correct prefix sampler must use nonnegative output-prefix
masses derived from the signed-pair sum; individual negative path contributions
are not probabilities. A follow-on preregistration should compare: dense
state-vector evolution, signed-pair counting, and causal-cone-restricted counting
on the same output-prefix/distribution contract, with small stabilizer,
non-Clifford, exact-destructive-interference and spectator-qubit families.
Record width, gates, Hadamard/path bits, signed counts, cancellation ratio when
defined, cone size, all count calls, and exact zero handling; never divide by a
zero final sum. Use exact arithmetic where supported and otherwise freeze
precision/tolerance in advance. Dense states are a baseline, not a universal
lower bound. Include compilation and rejected/timeout cases. This document
proposes that separate study; it does not report it as completed.

## Meaningful bounded conclusion
If the frozen tests pass, conclude only: on these 16 finite worlds, under the
specified fair-bit prior and observer policy, conditional rank generation
reproduces forward-execution transcript probabilities exactly and satisfies
(or fails) the declared host budget. This is a small constructive example of
emulation, with a standard general proof, not a proof that our universe is
simulated or that arbitrary simulation is computationally cheap.
